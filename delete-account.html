Delete your Cardana account

Request deletion of your account and associated data

To begin: email us from the address associated with your Cardana account. Use the subject “Cardana account deletion request.”

Email deletion request

If the button does not open your email app, send your request to life@creaturenetwork.dev.

What happens next

  1. We may reply to verify that you control the account.
  2. After verification, we will disable access and begin deletion.
  3. We aim to complete the request within 30 days and will confirm when it is complete.

Data covered by the request

The request covers account credentials and identities, profile information, uploaded profile and gallery media, follows and memberships, saved-event activity, posts and comments, reactions, direct messages, reports, notifications, and other data associated with the account.

Some records may be retained when legally required, necessary for fraud or abuse prevention, or needed to protect other users. Information in restricted backups may remain for up to 90 days before being overwritten. Content that must be retained may be de-identified so it is no longer associated with your account.

Deleting data without deleting your account

You may also email us to request deletion of specific content or other associated data without closing your account. Identify the content or data clearly so we can locate it.
