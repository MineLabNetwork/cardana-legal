Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cardana, operated by Jeremie Miller (“Cardana,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the Cardana mobile application and related services. Cardana is a social and discovery platform for trading-card-game collectors, event organizers, and vendors.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile information: email address, authentication provider, display name, profile photo, biography, public links, game interests, and account settings.
  • User content and activity: posts, comments, reactions, direct messages, uploaded photos and videos, reports, community memberships, follows, saved events, event participation, and resources you create or manage.
  • Location: with permission, precise location is accessed while the app is in use to show nearby events and calculate distance. Cardana does not intentionally retain your device’s precise location as part of your profile. Venue addresses and coordinates supplied by organizers may be stored and displayed.
  • Device and diagnostic information: operating-system type, error messages, redacted stack traces, coarse memory-use categories, session and security information, and advertising-related device information processed by Google Mobile Ads.
  • Media and permissions: camera, microphone, and selected photos or files are accessed only when you use features such as capturing, scanning, selecting, or uploading media. Notification access is used to deliver alerts you choose to receive.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; provide profiles, communities, messaging, feeds, event discovery, organizer and vendor tools; upload and display content; calculate nearby-event distances; provide notifications; prevent abuse; troubleshoot errors; maintain performance; comply with legal obligations; and show non-personalized advertising.

Service providers and disclosures

We do not sell personal information. We use service providers to operate Cardana, including Google Sign-In, Firebase, Google Mobile Ads, application hosting, database hosting, and object storage. These providers process information on our behalf or under their own terms to provide authentication, infrastructure, media storage, notifications, security, and non-personalized ads. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer.

Although Cardana requests non-personalized ads, Google may use contextual information and limited device information for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, security, and measurement.

Public content

Profile details and content you choose to publish may be visible to other users or the public. Direct messages are intended for their participants. Do not publish information you do not want others to see.

Data retention

We retain account information and user content while your account is active and as needed to provide Cardana. We may delete accounts after 24 months without a sign-in, ordinarily after providing at least 30 days’ notice when we have a valid contact address. Diagnostic reports are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to investigate reliability and security issues. Deleted information may remain in restricted backups for up to 90 days before being overwritten. We may retain limited records longer when required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

Your choices and deletion

You may decline optional permissions in your device settings, although related features may not work. You may request deletion of your account and associated data using our account deletion page. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. Some content may be retained in de-identified form or when legally required.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. Authentication tokens are stored using secure device storage, and optional biometric unlock is processed by your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Users aged 13 and older

Cardana is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Cardana changes. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or by email.
